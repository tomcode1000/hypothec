HYPOTHEC - THE GUARDIANS
========================
What the guardians (the code calls them "referees") are, how a loan's vault is made,
exactly what they check before they sign, what they refuse, how their log works, and
what they cannot do.

Written:   10 Oct 2026
Applies to: the build in this folder (services/, usdc/, core/, web/).
Related:   BRIEF.txt sections 4-5, docs/DECISIONS.md (D2, D4, D5, D8, D11, D12, D13)


------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT A GUARDIAN IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Every loan has a shielded Zcash vault held by FOUR keys: the borrower, the
  lender (the desk) and two guardians. Any 3 of the 4 keys can move it.
- A guardian is an independent operator company from the Hypothec operator list. It
  runs the Hypothec rules software, which holds the guardian's one key share and signs
  automatically, only when the published rules allow, and logs why.
- A guardian is NOT a person reading cases. Nobody at the operator clicks "approve".
  The rules are code; the operator's job is to run the service honestly, keep its key
  share safe, and be independent of the desk and the borrower.
- The borrower sees both guardians, with their addresses, before locking any ZEC, and
  agrees to them when it accepts the loan (D2). The acceptance is signed on Base and in
  the loanbook.
- In this build there are two operators, Guardian A and Guardian B, and the Hypothec
  team runs both (BRIEF section 7). The screens say so.

Who can move the vault (D4, 3 of 4):
  Borrower + both guardians      release after repayment
  Desk + both guardians          liquidation (price crash or deadline)
  Borrower + desk + one guardian cooperative close (e.g. a guardian is offline)
  Both guardians alone           CANNOT (2 of 4)
  Desk + one guardian            CANNOT (2 of 4)
So taking ZEC without the rules being met needs BOTH independent operators plus one
of the two parties to collude.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW THE VAULT IS MADE (ALL FOUR TOGETHER)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
It happens right after the borrower accepts. Each party runs its own party service
(core/: hypothec-party), which holds only that party's share. They talk through an
encrypted relay that holds no keys and cannot spend.

  1. The borrower's app opens a vault: "3 of 4", and gets an invite.
  2. Desk A's agent joins with the invite.
  3. Guardian A's service joins.
  4. Guardian B's service joins.
  5. The borrower seals the membership. All four compute a SAFETY NUMBER from the
     member list; it must be identical on all four. A different number means someone
     else is in the setup, and the setup stops.
  6. Key generation (FROST DKG): each party makes ITS OWN share of one key. The whole
     key is never assembled anywhere, not on any device, not at Hypothec, not ever.
  7. All four derive the same vault address. If any differs, the setup stops.

Each stage is written to the loan's log (vault-open, vault-join x3, safety-number,
vault) and shown live on My loan, in the "Making the vault" step.

In the demo all four services run on one machine (e2e/stack.sh). In the product the
desk's agent and the guardians' services are always-online servers; the borrower's
part runs in its app.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT A GUARDIAN WATCHES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every 3 seconds each guardian reads, for every loan it is named on:
  - the LOANBOOK: the lender's signed offer and the borrower's signed acceptance
    (terms, collateral, levels, both ZEC payout addresses), and the vault address;
  - the LOAN CONTRACT on the loan chain (Base Sepolia or Robinhood Chain testnet):
    status, repaid amount, deadline;
  - the VAULT, through ITS OWN party service and the vault's viewing key: address,
    3-of-4, balance (total and confirmed), and any spend proposed in it;
  - the PRICE FEED (section 7), only when a liquidation is proposed.
It never takes the desk's or the borrower's word for anything it can read itself.

Before acting on any loan it checks both signatures:
  - the offer is signed by the lender's Base address named in it;
  - the acceptance is signed by the borrower's Base address and refers to this exact
    offer (a digest of it).
If either fails it refuses the loan entirely and logs "lender or borrower signature
does not hold". This is what makes the loanbook tamper-proof: whoever runs it cannot
change terms or swap a payout address without breaking a signature.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. STAGE 1 - BEFORE THE BORROWER IS PAID (LOCK)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
When: the desk has funded the contract (status Funded). The USDC sits in the
contract, not with the borrower.

The guardian checks, all must hold:
  [ ] it is the vault all four made (its own view = the loanbook's vault address)
  [ ] the vault is 3 of 4
  [ ] the desk funded the contract (status Funded)
  [ ] the full collateral is in the vault (balance >= the collateral in the offer)
  [ ] it is CONFIRMED on the chain (confirmed balance >= collateral), not pending

Then it signs: it calls confirmLock(loan) on Base with its own Base key, and logs
"lock-confirmed" with the amount it saw and the transaction.
When BOTH guardians have confirmed, the contract itself pays the principal to the
borrower's payout address and the loan becomes Active.

While the ZEC has not arrived it logs once "waiting: vault has X of Y zat".

If no ZEC ever arrives: after the lock deadline (24 hours in the demo terms) the desk
cancels and the contract returns its USDC. If only part of the ZEC arrived, the
borrower takes it back: once the contract says Cancelled, the guardians sign its release
under the same checks as stage 2. Nobody loses money.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. STAGE 2 - BEFORE THE ZEC GOES BACK (RELEASE)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
When: the borrower has repaid in full through the contract (status Repaid), or the
loan was cancelled before the lock completed (status Cancelled: no USDC was ever paid
out), and asks for its ZEC back; its app proposes a spend from the vault and signs its
own share.

The guardian checks, all must hold:
  [ ] the contract says Repaid or Cancelled
  [ ] the spend has exactly one output
  [ ] that output pays the borrower's SIGNED payout address (from the acceptance)
  [ ] it returns everything except the fee (amount >= vault balance - 50,000 zat)

Then it approves the spend in the vault (signing its share) and logs
"approve-release" with the rule "repaid on Base". Borrower + both guardians = 3 of 4,
and the borrower's app sends it.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. STAGE 3 - BEFORE A LIQUIDATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
When: the loan is Active and the desk's agent proposes a liquidation spend.

First, a published rule must be met, judged with the guardian's own price:
  [ ] LTV >= the liquidation level in the signed offer (75% in the demo terms)
        LTV = debt still owed / (ZEC in the vault x ZEC price)
      OR the contract says the loan is past its deadline (isOverdue)
  If neither holds: refuse ("no liquidation rule is met at this price").
  If there is no price (fewer than 3 good exchanges): refuse.

Then the spend itself:
  [ ] it pays the desk's SIGNED ZEC address (from the offer) exactly once
  [ ] the desk gets AT MOST what it is owed at this price:
        share = owed / price x (1 + 2% slippage buffer), rounded up
  [ ] any second output pays the borrower's SIGNED address, and nothing else is paid
  [ ] desk share + borrower surplus >= vault balance - 50,000 zat
      i.e. the surplus goes back IN THE SAME TRANSACTION
  Underwater case: if the desk is owed more than the vault holds (the price fell
  straight through the line), the desk may take the vault less the fee and there is
  no surplus. Allowed only when the computed share really exceeds the vault (tested).

Then it approves (signs its share), logs "approve-liquidation" with the price, its
source, each exchange's average and the rule, e.g. "LTV 85.81% >= 75%". Desk + both
guardians = 3 of 4. Once the transaction is broadcast, each guardian calls
confirmLiquidation(loan) on Base and logs "liquidation-recorded"; when both have, the
contract marks the loan Liquidated.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. THE PRICE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Five exchanges: Bitstamp, Bitfinex, Gemini (ZEC/USD), Gate.io and MEXC (ZEC/USDT,
  USDT taken at par). (Coinbase, Kraken and Binance APIs did not resolve from the
  development machine.)
- Per exchange: the average of the last hour's 1-minute closes. An exchange counts
  only with at least 10 one-minute candles in the hour; a thinner one is left out and
  shown with the reason (on 9 Oct Bitfinex had 3).
- At least 3 exchanges must count, or there is no price and nothing that needs one
  happens. The price is the MEDIAN of those that count. Never a single tick.
- Read at most every 30 seconds (it is an hour average).
- Demo price override: for the video, the desk can set a price. The guardians use it,
  and every signature made under it is logged as "demo override". It does not exist
  in a production build.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. WHAT A GUARDIAN REFUSES (EXAMPLES)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - a liquidation while the loan is healthy (LTV under the line, not overdue)
  - a liquidation that pays the desk more than it is owed
  - a liquidation without the surplus to the borrower in the same transaction
  - any spend that pays an address that is not one of the two signed addresses
  - a release before the contract says Repaid (or Cancelled)
  - a release to any address other than the borrower's signed address
  - a release that keeps back more than the fee
  - anything on a loan whose offer or acceptance signature does not hold
  - anything in a vault that is not the agreed 3-of-4 vault
  - a liquidation when fewer than 3 exchanges give a price
Each refusal is logged once per spend and reason, with the reason.
(Tested: services/test/policy.test.ts, 11 tests.)


------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. THE SIGNED LOG
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every guardian decision is an entry in the loan's log in the loanbook, signed with
that guardian's Base key (EIP-191 personal_sign) over this exact text:

  Hypothec log:<kind>
  loan=<loan id>
  <field>=<value>        one line per field, fields sorted by name

Kinds a guardian writes:
  waiting               the vault does not yet hold the collateral
  lock-confirmed        saw the full amount; confirmed on Base (with the tx)
  approve-release       signed the release (rule: repaid, or cancelled, on Base)
  approve-liquidation   signed the liquidation (rule, price, source, each exchange)
  liquidation-recorded  recorded the liquidation on Base (Zcash tx + Base tx)
  refuse                refused, with the reason

Anyone can verify an entry: rebuild the text above from the entry's kind, loan and
data, and check the signature recovers to the guardian's Base address on the operator
list. The log carries no amounts in dollars or identities beyond the parties' Base
addresses; the ZEC figures are visible only to the loan's parties through the app.

The lender's and borrower's own entries (offer, accept) are signed the same way with
kinds "offer" and "accept". Setup notes (vault-open, vault-join, safety-number) and
the desk's "funded" and "liquidated" notes are unsigned notes.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. WHAT A GUARDIAN CANNOT DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Move the ZEC on its own, or with the other guardian alone (2 of 4).
- Pay the borrower's USDC before both guardians have confirmed the lock; pay it to a
  different address (the contract pays the address the borrower accepted with).
- Change where the ZEC goes: payout addresses are signed by the borrower and the desk
  and checked against those signatures.
- Hold the whole vault key: it never exists.
- See the borrower's wallet: the vault's viewing key shows the vault, nothing else.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. TRUST MODEL AND HONEST LIMITS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Residual trust: both guardians + the desk (or + the borrower) colluding could move
  the ZEC against the rules. Same model as Unchained's BTC loans, with a second
  independent guardian added. Say it openly.
- Liveness: release and liquidation need BOTH guardians online. If one is lost, the
  borrower + desk + the other guardian can still close; replacing a guardian (FROST
  share repair) is roadmap.
- The price feed is off-chain, read by each guardian itself.
- Demo: both guardians are run by the Hypothec team, on a local Zcash test chain
  (Zcash testnet is blocked by NU7 for now, D13); the price override exists only for
  the demo.
- Not audited. zafe (the vault and signing code underneath) is pre-release.
- Roadmap for real operators: independent companies (escrow, key-agent or audit firms,
  known Zcash organisations), a published operator list, a public signed log, and
  later a bond an operator loses if it signs against the rules.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. THE GUARDIANS SCREEN (web/ -> log.html)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- "What the guardians check, right now": the three stages above. Each check shows
  passed / waiting / failed with its figure (e.g. "0.0065 ZEC of 0.0065 ZEC"). Under
  each stage, each guardian's real decision from its signed log: confirmed or signed
  with the time and a link to the Base transaction, refused with the reason, watching,
  or nothing to sign yet.
  The checks are computed by the API with the same rule functions the guardians run
  (services/src/policy.ts), from Guardian A's own view of the vault; the decisions are
  the guardians' own signed entries.
- "The operators": each guardian's Base address, signed entries count, current seat,
  and the three rules it enforces.
- "Signed log": every entry, newest first, signed or note.
